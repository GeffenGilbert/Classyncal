Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Classyncal ("we", "our", "the app") is a tool that reads uploaded course syllabi and helps you add assignments, exams, and other dates to your Google Calendar and Google Tasks. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Syllabus content you upload. When you upload a syllabus, its content is sent to OpenAI's API to extract dates, assignments, and other schedule items into a structured format.
  • Google account data (via Google OAuth). When you sign in with Google, we request access to:
    • Google Calendar — to create calendar events for the items you approve.
    • Google Tasks — to create task entries for the items you approve.
    We do not request access to your email, contacts, files in Drive, or any other Google data beyond these scopes.
  • Authorization tokens. We store the OAuth token issued by Google so you don't have to sign in every time you use the app. It is encrypted at rest on our server and is used only to make the Calendar and Tasks API calls you initiate.
  • A session identifier. A cookie that lets us associate an upload with the browser that started it. It contains no personal information.

How we use this information

  • To parse your syllabus into a reviewable list of dates and assignments.
  • To create the calendar events and tasks you explicitly approve after reviewing the parsed content.
  • We do not use your Google data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the syllabus-to-calendar functionality described above.
  • We do not use your Google Workspace data (Calendar or Tasks) to train, improve, or fine-tune any AI or machine learning models, including third-party models such as OpenAI's.

How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit. All traffic between your browser, our servers, and Google's APIs is sent over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest. Your Google OAuth tokens are encrypted (AES-128 via Fernet, an authenticated symmetric encryption scheme) before being written to our database. The encryption key is stored separately from the database and its backups, so a copy of the database alone cannot be used to read a token.
  • Minimal scope. We request only the two narrow scopes this app needs — creating Calendar events and Tasks — never full account access, email, contacts, or Drive.
  • Restricted infrastructure access. Our database and job queue are not reachable from the public internet; only our own backend service can query them, over a private network with no externally-facing port.
  • Secure session cookies. The cookie identifying your browser session is HttpOnly (inaccessible to page scripts) and marked Secure in production, so it is only ever sent over HTTPS.
  • Minimal data model. We do not store your Google email, name, or profile information — only an internal identifier tied to your encrypted token.

How we share information

We do not sell or share your Google user data with third parties, except:

  • OpenAI — the content of your uploaded syllabus (not your Google account data) is sent to OpenAI's API for parsing, subject to OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • As required by law.

Data retention

  • The file you upload is held only in memory and in our processing queue while it is being parsed. It is never written to disk, and the queue is not persisted, so it does not survive a restart of our service.
  • The extracted result — the structured list of dates and assignments — is stored so you can review it, and is automatically deleted 24 hours after upload.
  • Your session expires after 30 days, at which point it and any associated records are deleted.
  • Your Google authorization token is retained until you revoke access or request deletion.

Your controls

  • You can revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page. Doing so deletes the token we hold.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold by contacting us at the email below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Classyncal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
